# DSP206 capture plan

Use the capture helper with:
- Model: `DSP206`
- Language: `fr` or `en`
- Port: `9761`

Capture priority:
1. Connection dump.
2. Gain, mute, phase.
3. Matrix routing and attenuation.
4. PEQ, HPF, LPF, GEQ.
5. Gate, Compressor, Limiter.
6. Delay.
7. Test Tone and Channel Name.

Keep raw captures local unless they are manually scrubbed.
